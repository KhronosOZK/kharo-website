About us

What Kharo does

We do not own the cars. We bring together the rental companies that do, hold them to the same standard, and make their prices properly comparable. Your agreement is with the operator. When something needs sorting out, you deal with us.

There are more licensed drivers in London than there are licensed vehicles, which is why a car is often harder to find than the work. Our job is to make the search shorter and the terms clearer.

Tracking

The tracker in your car

We would rather set this out plainly than leave you wondering. It is there for theft recovery and to protect the car. It is not there to watch you work.

Kharo can see

Live location, in three situations only. The car is reported stolen, the rental is in serious arrears, or you have asked us to help find it.

Ignition and mileage. For servicing intervals and the mileage cap.

Impact detection. So if you are in a collision we can ring you rather than wait to be told.

Your operator sees

Mileage, and whether the car is in use. Enough to manage servicing.

Not your live location. They do not get to watch where you are, where you stop or where you sleep. That is deliberate.

Kept for ninety days, then deleted. Never sold. Not to insurers, not to Uber or Bolt, not to anybody.

Ten days of conversation before anything happens

The tracker can stop the engine restarting. It can never stop a car that is moving, and it is never used while you have a passenger. A named manager has to authorise it and every use is logged. Talk to us and it does not happen.

The opportunity

More drivers than cars

London has 105,607 licensed private hire drivers and 92,895 licensed private hire vehicles. That gap of nearly thirteen thousand is the clearest evidence there is that demand for a car outstrips supply in this city.

Which raises an awkward question. If drivers are short of cars, why does any vehicle on your forecourt sit idle for a single day?

Usually because the two sides cannot find each other efficiently, and because the drivers who do turn up have not been checked, so a proportion of them stop paying in the second month. Both of those are solvable.

Protecting you

How we check drivers

You keep the final decision on every driver. What changes is the quality of what you are deciding on.

01

Eligibility, checked in seconds

The private hire badge is checked against the licensing register. We check the driving licence directly with the DVLA, with the driver's permission, so we are looking at the real record rather than a photograph of a card.

02

Identity, where the costliest fraud is caught

We check their documents are genuine and that the person holding them is the person applying, confirm their address, and look for duplicates across everyone already on Kharo.

03

Affordability, the check that matters most

With the driver's permission, we take a look at a year of their earnings from Uber and Bolt. What we care about most is not their average week but their quiet weeks, because that is when a driver falls behind.

04

Their record in the trade

How long they have been driving, references from previous operators and, in time, their payment history across the whole of Kharo. On a network, a default follows a driver.

We price risk rather than turning people away

Standard

Established record. One week deposit, weekly collection.

Higher deposit

Newer to the trade, or a thinner earnings record. Two to four weeks.

Twice weekly: collected twice a week rather than once, so your exposure never exceeds a few days of rent and trouble surfaces within 72 hours rather than 10 days.
