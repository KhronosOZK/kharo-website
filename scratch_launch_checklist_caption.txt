We vibecoded a private hire rental marketplace. Then, before a single driver got near it, we made ourselves do the boring checklist everyone skips.

Privacy policy: written, not templated. Terms of service: read, not skimmed. Cookie consent that actually blocks tracking until you say yes. A GDPR request flow that works, because "we'll sort it if someone asks" isn't a policy. Every image licensed. Meta tags and a sitemap so Google finds us. A 404 page with some personality. Accessibility checked, not just eyeballed. Rate limiting on every public endpoint. SPF and DKIM set up so our emails land in inboxes, not spam.

None of it is glamorous. All of it is the difference between a demo and a platform drivers and operators can trust with their money and their licence. Ship fast. Just don't skip the paperwork that keeps you shipped.
